# MLA paper

An MLA student-paper layout: US Letter, 12-point Times-family text, one-inch
margins, double spacing, a surname/page header, and a first-page heading.
The paper title is centered in regular type. Works Cited starts on a new page
and uses half-inch hanging indents.

Edit the name, surname, course, instructor, and due date in `main.tex`, your
argument in `sections/body.tex`, and source entries in `references.tex`.
Citations and references are maintained manually: alphabetize the entries and
supply actual page numbers for passages you cite. No Biber or additional
bibliography tool is needed. Replace the two demonstration sources.

Source: [MLA formatting guidance](https://style.mla.org/formatting-papers/).
Follow any additional instructions from your instructor.
