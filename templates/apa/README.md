# APA student paper (7th edition)

A student-paper layout with US Letter pages, 12-point Times-family text,
one-inch margins, double spacing, page numbers, a student title page, and
centered bold Level 1 headings. References starts on a new page with hanging
indents. An abstract and professional-paper running title are omitted by default.

Edit student, affiliation, course, instructor, and due date in `main.tex`;
write the paper in `sections/body.tex`. Adapt headings to your assignment.

Author–date citations and `references.tex` are maintained manually. Alphabetize
references, keep citations and entries consistent, and add actual locators for
quotations. The example book references follow APA 7 conventions; this template
does not use the older APA 6 `apacite` style or require Biber. Replace the two
demonstration sources. This is a student-paper starter, not an APA journal template.

Sources: [APA student-paper guide](https://apastyle.apa.org/instructional-aids/student-paper-setup-guide.pdf),
[APA book references](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/book-references).
Follow any additional instructions from your instructor.
