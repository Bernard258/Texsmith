\levelone{Method}
Explain your study design, materials, and procedure. Adapt the headings to
the kind of paper assigned; an essay need not follow an empirical-study outline.

\levelone{Results}
Present the evidence and findings without inventing measurements. Define
any terms needed to interpret the results.

\levelone{Discussion}
Interpret the findings, address limitations, and explain their implications.
